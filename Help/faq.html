Frequently asked questions

Can I compare multiple throws?

Yes. The results dialog keeps each valid throw and lets you compare launch conditions and distances.

What units does the app use?

The application uses metric values for the input and output values, such as meters and meters per second.

Does this replace a full sports analysis system?

No. The application is a focused projectile calculator and should be treated as a compact analysis tool rather than a full coach or biomechanical platform.
