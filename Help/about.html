About

Throwing App is developed to simulate throwing sports. It is a simple application to calculate and visualize the trajectory of thrown objects.

The application is based on the motion laws of the throwing movement. Air resistance has not been taken into account.

Let's examine the motion of an object thrown diagonally upwards from the ground. The piece then moves under the gravitational force of the earth. The acceleration of gravity g is directed towards the center of the earth, but changes so slowly on the surface of the earth that we can use the approximation g = 9.81 m/s2. So we assume that the acceleration has a constant value g = -gk where k is a unit vector pointing upwards. We do not take into account the rotation of the earth, in which case the movement takes place entirely in a plane. In this plane, the vector k and another unit vector i perpendicular to it, i.e. directed along the earth's surface, determine the orthogonal coordinate system. Integrating the equation g = -gk twice gives the equations for the object's speed v and the distance traveled by the object r

  • v = -gt k + v0
  • r = -1/2 gt2 k + tv0 + r0

where t is time, v0 is initial velocity and r0 is the starting location vector.
